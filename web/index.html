Deterministic Status Rules & Evaluation Logic

Progressing

Target is incomplete, current date is within deadline, and progress has been recorded within the 7-day inactivity window.

Completed

The cumulative progress value has reached or exceeded the target goal (≥ 100%), regardless of target date.

Stalled

Target is incomplete and within deadline, but no progress has been logged for 7 or more consecutive days.

Overdue

The target deadline has passed and the cumulative progress has not reached 100%.

Consistency Metric: Evaluated based on unique active logging days in the past 14-day rolling window. Requires at least 3 days of tracking history before reporting consistency to prevent premature or misleading scoring.
